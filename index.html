How to swim
  1. Start. On “Take your marks”, press and hold your kick key. Let go the moment the horn sounds. Letting go before the horn is a false start and you are out.
  2. Underwater. After the dive, tap kick for dolphin kicks. Press an arm key to break out and start swimming. You must be up by the 15 m mark.
  3. Arms. Alternate left and right at a steady pace. The same arm twice stalls you. A faster tempo is faster, but it drains stamina quickly.
  4. Kick. Tap kick once between strokes, while the timing cursor is in the green zone. Missing kicks breaks your rhythm.
  5. Breathe. Press breathe together with an arm stroke. A breath between strokes costs speed. Breathing when your lungs are still full wastes time.
  6. Turns. Near the wall, where the black line ends in a T, press kick to flip and push off. Flip too early or too late and you lose speed.
  7. Rhythm. Clean, even strokes with a kick in between fill the rhythm bar, which makes every stroke stronger.

# Swim Race

A rhythm swimming race in the browser. Dive on the horn, keep arms, kick and breathing in sync, flip at the wall, and race computer swimmers or a friend on the same keyboard.

Open `index.html` in a browser to play. No build step.

## Controls
| Action | Player 1 | Player 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left arm | A | J |
| Right arm | D | L |
| Kick / dive / flip | S | K |
| Breathe | W or Space | I |

Hold kick on "Take your marks", release on the horn. Alternate arms, kick once between strokes, breathe with a stroke, and press kick near the wall to flip.
